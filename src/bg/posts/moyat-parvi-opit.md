I remember reading a passage that talked about generosity, not just with money, but with time and attention. It seemed counter-intuitive in a busy world – give away what you feel you lack?

So, I decided to try a small experiment. For one week, I consciously focused on being more generous with my attention towards colleagues, even when I felt overwhelmed.

#### Observations

Surprisingly, by the end of the week, I *felt* less stressed, not more. Conversations felt more meaningful. A couple of colleagues spontaneously offered help on one of *my* tasks later that week. Coincidence? Maybe. But the shift in my own feeling of connection and reduced pressure was undeniable.
